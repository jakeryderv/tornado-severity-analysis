# Present: Slides, Questions & Discussion

## Purpose

The purpose of this presentation is to communicate the key findings from your unit assignment and demonstrate your ability to interpret and explain your experimental results.

Each student will:

- present their individual findings
- participate in class discussion

One student will also serve as the **Paper Lead** for each unit and present the assigned research paper.

The **Discussion Lead** is a separate graduate-student responsibility and is not part of this assignment.

---

## Individual Findings Presentation

Each student will deliver a **4–5 minute presentation** summarizing their work from the unit assignment.

Your presentation should address:

### 1. What you found

Briefly introduce:

- your dataset
- the problem you investigated
- the experiments you conducted
- your most important results

### 2. Your “aha” moment

Describe one concept, result, or observation that became clearer to you while completing the assignment.

### 3. What remains unclear

Identify at least one:

- question
- result
- concept

that you still find challenging or would like to understand better.

---

## Paper Lead

One student will serve as the **Paper Lead** for each unit.

Each student will serve in this role once during the semester.

The Paper Lead will deliver a **12–15 minute presentation** of the assigned research paper.

The presentation should address:

- the research problem and motivation
- the paper’s primary research question or objective
- the methodology or approach
- the main findings and evidence
- at least one limitation, unanswered question, or implication of the work

The Paper Lead should also be prepared to respond to questions from the class.

The Paper Lead slides are separate from the student’s individual findings slides.

Approximately **8–10 slides** are recommended.

Serving as Paper Lead does **not** replace the individual findings presentation. The Paper Lead must still complete the unit assignment and briefly present their own findings.

---

## Slide Requirements

Slides should be:

- clear
- concise
- designed for an academic presentation

Guidelines:

- use one primary idea per slide
- ensure all text and figures are readable from a distance
- do not include code on slides
- figures and tables should include appropriate titles, labels, or captions
- submit slides to Canvas as a PDF

---

## Class Participation

All students are expected to actively engage with the presentations and subsequent class discussion.

This may include:

- asking questions
- responding to questions
- comparing results across datasets or experiments
- connecting the research paper to findings presented by the class

The graduate-student Discussion Lead role is assessed separately.

---

## Submission

Submit your **individual presentation slides as a PDF** through Canvas before the beginning of class on the presentation day.

If you are the assigned Paper Lead, also submit your **Paper Lead presentation slides as a PDF**.

No code will be executed during the presentation.

All experiments and analyses should be completed before class.
